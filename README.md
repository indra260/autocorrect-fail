## The entry

> Mom, my rapist says I'm doing really well and I should call you more — I mean my THERAPIST. THERAPIST!!

A text to Mom about therapy, ruined by the most unfortunate word-split
autocorrect can produce: "therapist" becomes "the rapist", and the frantic
all-caps correction only makes it read worse.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes that single line to
`/output/entry.txt` when run:
